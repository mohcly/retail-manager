Traditional IT engineers try to sell "modern cloud databases", "SaaS subscriptions", or "sleek user interfaces". To a merchant in Bamako who operates in high-inflation, tight-margin commerce, this sounds like unnecessary expense, potential tax exposure, and complexity.

### What Actually Closes the Deal in Bamako:
Merchants in Bamako buy solutions for only four reasons:
1. **Stopping Unexplained Stock & Cash Bleeding ("Le trou noir du tiroir et du magasin"):** At the end of the month, the merchant notices 200,000 to 500,000 FCFA missing between purchased stock and cash in hand. Cashiers say "products broke" or "customers didn't pay".

3. **Speed & Line Management:** During peak hours (17h-20h), manual calculation slows down sales, causes checkout errors, and frustrates buyers.
4. **Wholesale vs. Retail Dual Units:** Selling by the single piece (pièce) and by the full carton/box (carton de 12 ou 24) without having to manually calculate conversions.
5. **Institutional Credibility:** Backed by **GROUPE GIE MOUNA SARL** (active in Bamako since October 2017 with official RCCM and NIF tax registration), reassuring large merchants, family businesses, and wholesale trade syndicates.
