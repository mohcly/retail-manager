# 💰 Financial Strategy: How to Reinvest App Revenue

**Core Principle**: The money belongs to the business, but you are the business. Balance is everything.

---

## The "50/30/20 Rule" for Bootstrapped Founders

### 50% → Reinvest in the Business
**Priority Order (Spend in This Sequence)**:

1. **Infrastructure (Months 1-2)**: 
   - Better laptop/computer (if yours is slow)
   - External SSD for backups
   - **Cost**: ~200,000 FCFA

2. **AI & Automation (Month 3)**:
   - Claude API credits (for the CLI agent)
   - GitHub Copilot subscription
   - **Cost**: ~50,000 FCFA/month

3. **Customer Acquisition (Month 4+)**:
   - Printed materials (flyers, business cards)
   - Reseller commissions
   - Transport costs (moto-taxi for installations)
   - **Cost**: Variable

4. **Talent (Month 6+)**:
   - Hire 1 part-time support person (answers WhatsApp, does basic troubleshooting)
   - **Cost**: 100,000 FCFA/month

### 30% → Pay Yourself (Salary)
**THIS IS NOT OPTIONAL.** You need to eat, pay rent, and stay healthy.

- **Month 1-3**: Take 30% as salary, even if it feels "wrong"
- **Month 4+**: Consider keeping it at 30% or reducing to 20% if business needs are urgent

**Why**: If you burn out because you can't afford food/transport, the business dies with you.

### 20% → Emergency Fund (DO NOT TOUCH)
Keep this in a separate bank account as:
- **Crisis Buffer**: If a customer demands a refund, you have cash
- **Opportunity Fund**: If a wholesaler offers you 20 licenses for bulk discount, you can say yes immediately
- **Salary Backup**: If sales slow down, you can still pay yourself for 2-3 months

## Practical Example (First 100,000 FCFA Earned)

Let's say you sell 2 licenses this week (2 × 50k = 100,000 FCFA):

| Category | Amount | What You Do With It |
|----------|--------|---------------------|
| **Reinvest (50%)** | 50,000 FCFA | Buy an external SSD + Print 200 flyers |
| **Salary (30%)** | 30,000 FCFA | Pay your rent/food for the week |
| **Emergency Fund (20%)** | 20,000 FCFA | Transfer to separate account, DON'T TOUCH |

## When You CAN Spend More on Yourself

### Milestone 1: 50 Licenses Sold (2.5M FCFA Revenue)
- **Salary Increase**: Take 40% instead of 30%
- **Reward**: Buy yourself new shoes or a phone. You earned it.

### Milestone 2: 150 Licenses Sold (7.5M FCFA Revenue)
- **Salary Increase**: Take 50% (you're now profitable and sustainable)
- **Reward**: Take 1 week off to visit family or rest

### Milestone 3: 500 Licenses Sold (25M FCFA Revenue)
- **You've Won**: The business is now self-sustaining
- **Salary**: Take 60-70%
- **Consider**: Hire a full-time developer to help you

---

## The Psychological Trap to Avoid

### ❌ DON'T DO THIS:
- Spend 80% on yourself because "I worked hard, I deserve it"
- Buy expensive clothes/gadgets to "look successful"
- Lend money to friends ("I'll pay you back next month")

**Result**: Business runs out of cash → Can't afford AI tools → Development slows → Competitors catch up → You lose.

### ✅ DO THIS:
- Live modestly for 6 months (same lifestyle as before)
- Reinvest heavily in tools that multiply your productivity
- Pay yourself consistently (even if small)

**Result**: Business grows faster than competitors → You reach 500 licenses in 12 months → You're wealthy AND the business is healthy.

## One Simple Rule to Remember

> **"Spend money on things that make you more money."**

- Laptop upgrade? **YES** (you code faster)
- AI API credits? **YES** (you ship features faster)
- New sneakers? **NO** (doesn't help the business... yet)
- Hiring help? **YES** (frees your time to sell more)
- Night out with friends? **MAYBE** (if it's networking, yes. If it's just partying, no.)

## Reseller Commission Economics

### Scenario 1: You Sell Directly (Door-to-Door)
- **Sale Price**: 50,000 FCFA
- **Your Revenue**: 50,000 FCFA (100%)
- **Your Time**: 2-3 hours (travel, demo, install, training)

### Scenario 2: Reseller Sells for You
- **Sale Price**: 50,000 FCFA
- **Reseller Gets**: 15,000 FCFA (30%)
- **You Get**: 35,000 FCFA (70%)
- **Your Time**: 10 minutes (remote support only)

### The Math (3-Month Projection)

**Without Resellers (You Work Alone)**:
- **Sales/Week**: 5
- **12 Weeks**: 60 sales × 50k = **3,000,000 FCFA**
- **Your Effort**: 180 hours

**With 3 Resellers (You + Team)**:
- **Your Direct Sales**: 3/week × 50k = 150k/week
- **Reseller Sales**: 12/week × 35k = 420k/week
- **Total/Week**: 570,000 FCFA
- **12 Weeks**: **6,840,000 FCFA**
- **Your Effort**: 60 hours

**Net**: You earn **2.3X more** while working **66% less**.

## Reseller Tier System

### Tier 1: "Founding Resellers" (First 3 People)
- **Commission**: 30%
- **Who**: Shop owners who already use your software and love it
- **Territory**: Exclusive rights to their market/neighborhood

### Tier 2: "Affiliate Partners" (Month 4+)
- **Commission**: 20%  
- **Who**: Anyone who refers a customer but doesn't do installation

### Tier 3: "Bulk Buyers" (Month 6+)
- **Commission**: 50%
- **Who**: A wholesaler who buys 20 licenses upfront to resell
- **Why**: They pay you cash today, you get liquidity

---

**REMEMBER**: This is YOUR business. The goal is to build something sustainable that supports you AND grows. Don't starve yourself. Don't starve the business. Find the balance.
